# Bank reconciliation code review — 2026-11-11

Scope: `BankReconciliationService`, `BankReconciliationController`, treasury models, `BankReconciliationTest`, `web/src/pages/Reconciliation.tsx`.
Reference: Odoo bank reconciliation and Zoho Books banking (decisions closed 2026-11-11; no new accounting decisions were made). Branch: `agent/bank-reconciliation-review`.

Severity: High = wrong balances or double posting; Medium = wrong/blocked results in realistic data, or unbounded failure; Low = polish or edge case.

| # | Finding | Severity | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Book side used base amounts (`base_debit_minor`/`base_credit_minor`), so a foreign bank account would be compared in EGP against a USD statement; session creation therefore refused every foreign account ("DEC-037"). Odoo/Zoho reconcile a foreign bank account in its own currency. | High | Fixed | `bookLines`, `bookOpeningBalance`, `match` now use the account's native `debit_minor`/`credit_minor`; the base-only restriction is removed. Tests `test_foreign_currency_bank_account_reconciles_in_its_own_currency`, `test_foreign_currency_session_opening_balance_is_native` |
| 2 | Revaluation base-only lines (zero transaction amount) and legacy base-currency lines on a foreign account would show as zero or phantom outstanding items and corrupt the native opening balance. | High | Fixed | Lines of a foreign account are kept only if `currency_code` = account currency and a native amount > 0; the count of skipped lines is returned as `summary.excluded_non_native_lines` and shown in the UI. Same test as #1 |
| 3 | A cancelled receipt / supplier payment and its reversal entry both appeared as unmatched ledger lines (a phantom deposit in transit plus a phantom unpresented payment, not matchable to anything). | High | Fixed | `voidedLineIds` pairs an entry with its `reversal_of_id` reversal (equal and opposite native amounts, neither matched) and marks both `reversed`: kept in the book balance (net zero), removed from outstanding items, differences and candidates, and refused by `match`. If the original was already matched to a bank line, the reversal correctly stays an unpresented payment. Tests `test_a_receipt_and_its_reversal_pair_out_of_the_outstanding_items`, `test_reversal_of_a_matched_receipt_stays_an_outstanding_payment` |
| 4 | An approved session was recomputed live on every view. A later session that matched one of its deposits in transit changed the "frozen" figures (the snapshot was stored but never served). | High | Fixed | `compute` serves the stored snapshot (refreshed only with header fields) for approved sessions. Test `test_approved_session_keeps_its_snapshot_when_later_sessions_match_its_items` |
| 5 | The same unrecorded bank line could be proposed again and again as an adjustment voucher (nothing tracked which lines a voucher covers), so a bank charge could be posted twice. | High | Fixed | New nullable column `bank_reconciliations.adjustment_line_map` (additive migration, working `down()`); covered lines are excluded/refused while the voucher is live (not rejected/cancelled/voided or undone by a posted correction). Test `test_the_same_bank_line_cannot_get_two_adjustment_vouchers` |
| 6 | Approving a session while a proposed voucher was still draft/submitted left a document that could be posted after the lock, adding an item the snapshot does not contain. | Medium | Fixed | `approve` refuses while any linked voucher is draft, submitted or approved-not-posted. Test `test_unposted_adjustment_voucher_blocks_approval` |
| 7 | An approved session could never be corrected: its matches stay consumed forever, yet the UI told users to "create a new session". Odoo ("reset") and Zoho ("undo reconciliation") allow this. | Medium | Fixed | New `POST reconciliations/{id}/reopen` (permission `reconciliation.approve`, reason required, newest approved session of the account only, snapshot kept in the audit event `bank_reconciliation.reopened`); UI button and modal. Test `test_approved_session_can_be_reopened_with_a_reason_newest_first` |
| 8 | Slash dates without a format were read with Carbon's US month/day order (03/04/2026 became 4 March); unparseable dates raised an uncaught `InvalidFormatException` (HTTP 500). | Medium | Fixed | Ambiguous day/month values are refused with a message asking for the date format; unambiguous day-first values are read correctly; unreadable dates give a 422 naming the row. Tests `test_import_rejects_ambiguous_slash_dates_without_a_format`, `test_import_reports_an_unreadable_date_with_its_row_instead_of_failing` |
| 9 | Amount parsing: text such as "n/a" silently became 0; Arabic-Indic digits/separators were stripped to nothing (0); a trailing minus ("50.00-") lost its sign; spreadsheet float noise ("12.339999999999998") was truncated to 12.33. | Medium | Fixed | `parseAmount`/`latinDigits`/`roundToExponent` (string arithmetic, no floats); zero/blank rows are skipped. Tests `test_import_reads_arabic_digits_semicolons_trailing_minus_and_spreadsheet_floats`, `test_import_rejects_text_in_the_amount_column` |
| 10 | CSV in Windows-1256 (common for Egyptian banks) or UTF-16 produced invalid UTF-8 and a database error; descriptions longer than 255 characters failed the insert (PostgreSQL varchar(255)); adjustment-voucher line text had the same limit. | Medium | Fixed | `toUtf8` converts UTF-16/CP1256; text columns truncated to 255; voucher descriptions to 250. Test `test_import_converts_windows_1256_and_truncates_long_text` |
| 11 | XLSX serial dates failed whenever a date format was configured (the UI always sends `Y-m-d`). | Medium | Fixed | Serial dates are accepted regardless of the format. Test `test_import_accepts_excel_serial_dates_even_with_a_date_format` |
| 12 | "Huge files": no row cap (20 MB CSV is hundreds of thousands of rows inserted one by one in one transaction); XLSX parts were read without an uncompressed-size limit (zip bomb). | Medium | Fixed | `MAX_STATEMENT_ROWS = 20000` with a message to split by month; XLSX sheet/shared-strings capped at 40 MB uncompressed; XML parsed with `LIBXML_NONET`. Test `test_import_refuses_files_with_too_many_rows` |
| 13 | Auto-match candidate search was O(bank lines x ledger lines) with a `Carbon::parse` per pair; already-matched lines with a tolerance residual could be paired a second time; ordering relied on sort stability. | Medium | Fixed | Ledger lines indexed by direction and remaining amount; candidates only from untouched lines; fully ordered sort (ids last) so the result is deterministic. Existing auto-match / ambiguity tests still pass |
| 14 | Concurrent matching in two sessions of the same account could both consume the same ledger line (consumption is global but only the session row was locked). | Medium | Fixed | Matching, auto-match, unmatch and propose lock the cash account row for the transaction (`lockAccount`). No dedicated test (needs real concurrency) |
| 15 | Open sessions kept the opening book balance captured at creation; a back-dated posting before the period start made the closing book balance wrong. | Medium | Fixed | Open sessions recompute the opening balance on every read. |
| 16 | Session list returned the full approval snapshot (all lines) of every session. | Low | Fixed | `toApiArray(false)` for the list. Test `test_list_endpoint_omits_the_snapshot_payload` |
| 17 | The offset account of an adjustment voucher could be the bank account itself. | Low | Fixed | Refused with a message. Test `test_the_same_bank_line_cannot_get_two_adjustment_vouchers` |
| 18 | `;` and tab separated files (regional Excel exports) failed with "column not found" when the default `,` delimiter was sent. | Low | Fixed | Delimiter auto-detected when the header collapses to one cell. Test in #9 |
| 19 | UI: opening/closing balances were converted with a hard-coded `EGP` exponent (wrong for a USD/KWD account); many-to-many selections paired lines by index and could send an invalid/crashing payload; "Approved at" printed for open sessions; the voucher modal offered no way to choose the charges/interest account although the API supports it; the approve dialog stated it can never be reopened. | Medium | Fixed | Account currency used; `allocateMatch` sweep (vitest `reconciliationAllocation.test.ts`); optional account-code fields; reopen dialog; client-side 20 MB check |
| 20 | Maker-checker: approve needs `reconciliation.approve` (separate from `manage`) but the approver may be the creator. Odoo/Zoho have no approval step; DEC-017 keeps approvals optional. | Low | Open (by design) | No change. No reconciliation-specific segregation setting exists (only `documents.segregation`, `expenses.segregation`, `accounting.refund_segregation`), so there is nothing to honour; the adjustment voucher itself keeps its maker-checker. Re-checked 2026-11-12 (open-items pass). |
| 21 | A bank line dated just before the period end whose ledger entry is posted after `period_to` cannot be matched in that session (ledger lines are limited to `period_to`), so it blocks approval as "unrecorded". | Medium | Fixed | Look-ahead window, setting `treasury.reconciliation_lookahead_days` (default 31, validated 0-366 in the settings API, 0 disables). Ledger lines posted up to that many days after `period_to` are loaded for matching only (`after_period_end`, shown in the UI as "dated after period end"). They never enter `book_closing_minor`, deposits in transit or outstanding payments; a look-ahead line matched in the session is added to the adjusted book side as `recorded_after_period_minor` (so the difference stays zero and the approved snapshot is frozen with it). Consumption is global, so a later session sees the line as already matched (remaining 0) and cannot reuse it; a reversal posted after period end does not hide the original receipt of the period. Tests `test_ledger_line_posted_after_period_end_matches_but_never_enters_the_period_balance`, `test_look_ahead_window_is_a_validated_setting_and_zero_disables_it`, `test_a_look_ahead_reversal_does_not_hide_the_outstanding_receipt_of_the_period` |
| 22 | Statement opening/closing balances default to 0 / total movement when not supplied, and a session whose period differs from its statement's still uses the statement balances. The difference is shown and blocks approval, so it is not silent. | Low | Fixed | No silent defaults. Import: opening = entered value, else the file's running-balance column, else the previous approved session's closing (Odoo); closing = entered value or last running balance; otherwise 422. Session: entered values, else the statement's balances only when the session covers exactly the statement period, else previous approved closing / statement line balance; otherwise 422. The origin is stored (`balance_source`, additive migration with working `down()`), returned by the API and shown in the UI. Approval is blocked for sessions with no recorded source (legacy) or an unverified legacy statement whose balances do not agree with the statement movement. Tests `test_statement_import_requires_explicit_balances`, `test_statement_balances_come_from_the_balance_column_or_the_previous_approved_session`, `test_opening_balance_is_carried_from_the_previous_approved_session`, `test_session_requires_bank_balances_and_shows_where_they_came_from`, `test_approval_is_blocked_when_the_bank_balances_were_never_confirmed` |
| 23 | No export of reconciliation data exists, so there is no formula-injection exposure there; imported text is rendered as escaped text by React. Any future export must use `ReportCsv`, which already neutralises formula prefixes. Voucher text copied from bank narratives flows into documents, which export through `ReportCsv`. | Low | Open (no action) | `app/Modules/Reports/Services/ReportCsv.php` |
| 24 | Auto-match never picks among ambiguous equal-amount candidates, even when only one has a matching bank reference (Odoo prefers the reference). | Low | Fixed | Equal-amount candidates are scored by evidence (bank reference vs entry number 4, vs memo/line description 3, bank description contains entry number 2, shared word of 4+ characters 1). A pair is auto-matched only when it is strictly the best for both its bank line and its ledger line; ties stay ambiguous and manual. Tests `test_equal_amount_candidates_are_disambiguated_by_reference_and_ties_stay_manual`, `test_equal_amount_candidates_with_the_same_evidence_stay_ambiguous`, `test_description_words_disambiguate_when_there_is_no_reference`; existing `test_ambiguous_matches_require_human_review` unchanged |
| 25 | Overlapping sessions for the same account are allowed (consumption is global so double matching is impossible); matching against entries of closed fiscal periods is allowed because matching never writes to the ledger. An adjustment voucher dated in a closed period fails only when posted (user can change the date). | Low | Open (by design) | — |
| 26 | Period-end checklist counts open sessions overlapping the period; it does not flag a bank account with no session at all. | Low | Fixed | New non-blocking check `unreconciled_bank_accounts` (warning, never blocks `can_close`): active `bank` accounts with posted activity up to period end and no session (any status) overlapping the period. Test `test_period_end_checklist_warns_about_bank_accounts_without_a_reconciliation` |
