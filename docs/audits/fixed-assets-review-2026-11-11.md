# Fixed-assets module review, 2026-11-11

Scope: `FixedAssetService`, `FixedAssetReportService`, models, controllers, `FixedAssetTest`, `web/src/pages/FixedAssets.tsx`, after party subaccounts (ADR-0015), realized FX, mixed-currency posting lines, the 1150 split, per-account classification, `RoundingPolicy`, branch numbering and closed-period reversal dating.

Reference: Odoo (Assets) and Zoho Books (Fixed Assets). Accounting decisions are closed (`docs/product/decisions-closed-2026-11-11.md`, DEC-039 straight-line monthly, DEC-010 rounding); no new decision was invented. Where the code disagreed with the reference it was changed to follow it and the PR says so.

Severity: High = wrong ledger or register figures; Medium = wrong in a realistic scenario or a missing control; Low = cosmetic, UX or rare.

| # | Finding | Severity | Status | Evidence |
|---|---------|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Foreign-currency asset was capitalized in base only. The offset (payable) carried no currency, so revaluation and realized FX at settlement ignored it. | High | Fixed | `capitalize()` now posts cost at the historical base amount (non-monetary) and the offset `PostingLine::credit(...)->in(currency, rate)`. `test_foreign_currency_asset_keeps_the_offset_in_its_currency_and_cost_in_base` |
| 2 | The capitalization credit went to the AP control account (2100) with no party, so the control account no longer equalled the sum of supplier subaccounts (ADR-0015). | High | Fixed | With a supplier and the AP account as offset, the credit goes to the supplier's payable subaccount tagged `supplier` (same test). |
| 3 | Double recognition: an asset bought through an expense/bill that already debited the cost account was capitalized again (Dr cost / Cr offset). | High | Fixed | New optional `acquisition_journal_entry_id` (additive migration). Capitalization links the booked entry and posts nothing; the entry must be posted, not reversed and cover the cost, and a bill cannot back more than it debited. `test_an_asset_linked_to_a_booked_bill_does_not_post_the_cost_twice`, `test_a_bill_cannot_back_more_cost_than_it_booked` |
| 4 | Disposal ignored the disposal month and the date: depreciation was not charged up to the disposal date (Odoo and Zoho do), missing earlier months were silently skipped, and a date before an already posted month-end run was accepted (accumulated beyond the date, wrong gain/loss). | High | Fixed | Disposal now requires earlier months to be posted, refuses a date before the last posted period end, and charges the disposal month pro rata inside the disposal entry (`disposal_depreciation_minor`, reports updated). `test_disposal_depreciates_to_the_disposal_date`, `test_disposal_requires_earlier_months_to_be_depreciated_and_a_sensible_date` |
| 5 | Impairment did not reset the schedule: monthly depreciation kept the pre-impairment rate, so the asset reached residual early and the schedule (which subtracted the full impairment from every row) was wrong. | Medium | Fixed | After an applied impairment the remaining depreciable amount is re-spread over the remaining months (Odoo recomputes after a revaluation). Schedule shows posted months from the items and projects the rest. `test_impairment_respreads_the_remaining_amount_over_the_remaining_months` |
| 6 | Rounding policy not applied: monthly amount used `intdiv` (floor) and the day proration was hard-coded half-up. | Medium | Fixed | `Money::divideRounded` (integer half-up / bankers); used for monthly, prorate_days, impairment re-spread and the disposal charge. The last period still absorbs the residual. `test_monthly_amount_uses_the_rounding_policy_and_the_total_is_exact`, `test_divide_rounded_follows_the_rounding_policy` |
| 7 | A posted depreciation run could not be corrected (no reversal; delete refused). A wrong run left the register and GL stuck. | Medium | Fixed | `POST /depreciation-runs/{run}/reverse` (`assets.approve`): reverses the journal through `PostingService` (closed-period dating applies), restores the register, releases the asset-month claims, status `reversed`. Only the latest period of each asset; not after disposal. A reversed run cannot be posted or deleted. UI button added. `test_a_posted_run_can_be_reversed_and_run_again`, `test_only_the_latest_period_of_an_asset_can_be_reversed` |
| 8 | Reconciliation compared each category with the whole GL balance of its account. Categories sharing accounts (the defaults) showed false differences. | Medium | Fixed | Comparison is per distinct account; `accounts` array added; category rows show null GL figures when the account is shared. `test_reconciliation_handles_shared_accounts_and_unregistered_fixed_asset_balances` |
| 9 | Ledger balances on accounts classified `fixed_asset` with no register category (bill or opening balance booked straight to a cost account, party-less lines) were invisible. | Medium | Fixed | `unregistered_accounts` listed and counted in the cost difference (same test). |
| 10 | Draft (never capitalized) assets were counted in roll-forward additions, carrying values, movements and the reconciliation. | Medium | Fixed | Reports exclude `draft`. `test_reconciliation_as_of_a_date_ignores_drafts_and_later_depreciation` |
| 11 | Reconciliation and carrying values "as of" a date used today's accumulated depreciation and included the month-end run of a mid-month date. | Medium | Fixed | Register side uses depreciation posted by that date (month-end run date), same test. |
| 12 | Draft edit: changing the currency kept the previous rate (e.g. EGP -> USD at rate 1). Rate source was always `market` even for a manual rate. | Medium | Fixed | Stale rate dropped on currency change; market rate re-read on date change; manual rate kept and labelled `manual`. `test_changing_the_currency_of_a_draft_does_not_reuse_the_old_rate` |
| 13 | A post-time branch check was missing: an asset transferred after a draft run was created would be depreciated on the old branch's journal. | Medium | Fixed | Item dropped at posting when the asset's branch differs from the run branch. |
| 14 | Category accounts were not type-checked (an expense account could be the cost account); duplicate category or asset code returned a 500 (unique violation). | Medium | Fixed | Cost/accumulated must be active postable asset accounts, expense account an expense account; duplicates give a 422. `test_category_accounts_must_have_the_right_types` |
| 15 | Disposal proceeds account was any account code (e.g. an expense account). | Medium | Fixed | Must be an active, postable asset account other than the category cost/accumulated accounts. `test_disposal_proceeds_must_go_to_an_asset_account` |
| 16 | FX comparison used `(float)`. | Low | Fixed | `bccomp`. |
| 17 | UI: "scrap" labelled with a wrong Arabic word; run-created message mixed amount and count; reconciliation could not show null/shared figures; errors were hidden behind open modals; no client validation; no FX-rate input; posted runs offered "Approve & post". | Low | Fixed | `FixedAssets.tsx`: label, message, accounts and unregistered tables, in-modal error banners, validation (life, currency, rate, dates, residual), exchange-rate and booked-journal fields, reverse action with reason. |
| 18 | Branch transfer creates no journal. Cost and accumulated depreciation stay on the original branch in the GL while later depreciation and the disposal post on the new branch, so branch balance sheets (not branch P&L going forward) are distorted. The global reconciliation is unaffected. | Medium | Open | Deliberate in code and documented in the reconciliation note and `test_branch_transfer_preserves_global_totals_and_records_audit_trail`. A fix needs an inter-branch clearing account policy (two entries, one per branch), which is an accounting decision; not invented here. |
| 19 | Impairment is applied to the register by a separate manual step after the voucher is posted; between posting and applying, GL and register differ (reconciliation shows it). The voucher lines are not verified against the asset's accumulated account. | Low | Open | `applyImpairment()`. |
| 20 | Draft assets cannot be edited from the UI (the API supports it). Disposal `notes` is accepted but not stored. | Low | Open | `FixedAssets.tsx`, `dispose()`. |
| 21 | Runs can be created for future periods and out of order; later catch-up takes the remainder in one month. With `prorate_days` the first month is partial and the last month absorbs the difference (life periods, not life + 1). | Low | Open | `amountForPeriod()`. Matches the closed DEC-039 wording (straight-line monthly); documented. |
| 22 | Foreign-currency disposal proceeds are entered in base only; gain/loss is base (correct for a non-monetary asset) but a proceeds account with a fixed foreign currency is refused by `PostingService`. | Low | Open | `dispose()`. |
| 23 | No server-side CSV/PDF export for the fixed-asset reports (the UI shows tables only). | Low | Open | No export route under `reports/fixed-assets`. |
| 24 | Transfer is allowed to a branch outside the actor's branch scope (the actor loses access afterwards). | Low | Open | `transfer()`, `FixedAssetController::transfer`. |

Compatibility: one additive migration (`2026_11_19_000100_add_fixed_asset_review_columns`: nullable `acquisition_journal_entry_id`, `disposal_depreciation_minor` default 0) with a working `down()` (verified up, rollback, up on SQLite). The API only gains fields, one route and the `reversed` run status; existing payload fields are unchanged except the reconciliation category rows, whose GL/difference figures are `null` when the account is shared.
